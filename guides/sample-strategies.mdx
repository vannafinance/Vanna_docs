These are testnet workflow examples for combining Vanna borrowing, Earn, and external supply. They are not promises of positive carry, fixed yield, or price neutrality.

- [Cross-Pool Rate Carry](/guides/strategies/cross-pool-rate-carry): borrow a token and supply the matching Blend asset.
- [Earn, Borrow, and Hedge](/guides/strategies/earn-borrow-hedge): separate an Earn holding from a margin strategy and evaluate its exposure.
- [Parallel Rate Carry](/guides/strategies/parallel-rate-carry): manage more than one matching-asset borrow/supply leg.
- [Dual Yield Stack](/guides/strategies/dual-yield-stack): hold Earn receipts while managing a separate Farm position.

Use the exact token variant, current rates, and account-wide health for every leg. All margin strategies in one SmartAccount share debt and liquidation risk. Earn receipts held in the wallet do not automatically collateralize that account.

The Stellar application supports Spot, not perpetual/options trading. A hedge requiring an unavailable derivative cannot be executed through those redirected routes.
