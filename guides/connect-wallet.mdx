## Step 1 — Set Up a Stellar Wallet

Vanna supports any Stellar-compatible wallet. If you don't have one yet, here are a few popular options:

- **Freighter** — [freighter.app](https://freighter.app) (browser extension)
- **LOBSTR** — [lobstr.co](https://lobstr.co) (mobile + web)
- **xBull** — [xbull.app](https://xbull.app) (browser extension)

Once installed, create a new account or import an existing one using your secret key or recovery phrase.

<Warning>
  Keep your recovery phrase safe and offline. Vanna will never ask for it.
</Warning>

## Step 2 — Connect to Vanna

Navigate to the Vanna Product website. Click on **Launch App** on the homepage. Once the app launches, click **Connect Wallet** in the top-right corner.

Select your wallet from the connection modal and approve the request. Once approved, your Stellar address will appear in the top-right corner — you are now connected.

## Step 3 — Get Testnet Tokens (Testnet only)

If you want to try Vanna without using real funds, switch to testnet first:

1. Open your wallet and switch the network to **Testnet**. Return to the Vanna app — it detects the change automatically.

2. Click the **Faucet** button in the top navigation bar to open the faucet modal.

3. Click **Mint** next to the tokens you need, or click **Mint All** to claim all available test tokens at once. Each mint requires a wallet signature.

<Warning>
  Always keep a small XLM balance in your wallet. Every Stellar transaction requires a small XLM network fee — if your XLM balance reaches zero, all transactions will fail.
</Warning>
