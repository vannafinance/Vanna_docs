description: "Illustrative testnet workflow and its accounting limits."
mode: "wide"
---

Supply a chosen wallet asset in Earn, then separately manage collateral and borrowing in Margin. The Earn position and SmartAccount use different custody and accounting.

Any hedge must specify the exposure being offset and the actual spot assets held or owed. Holding an Earn receipt while borrowing another asset does not by itself create a hedge. Perpetual and options trading are not supported in this Stellar build.

Review both pool liquidity and margin health. To exit, redeem Earn receipts subject to payout caps and unwind/repay the margin leg independently.
