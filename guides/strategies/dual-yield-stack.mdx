title: "Dual Yield Stack"
description: "Illustrative testnet workflow and its accounting limits."
mode: "wide"
---

Hold an Earn position in your wallet while running a separate margin-funded Blend supply position. The two yield sources have different custody, rates, withdrawal limits, and risks.

Earn receipts do not automatically back the margin position. A matching borrowed/supplied Blend leg can offset some token exposure, but your equity, changing rates, and external-protocol exposure remain. Combined yield is not guaranteed positive or price-neutral.

Manage the Earn leg through its pool and the Farm leg through the SmartAccount. Redeeming Earn may partially fill; withdrawing Farm must be followed by debt repayment where applicable.

See [Sample Strategies](/guides/sample-strategies), [Borrow](/guides/margin/borrow), and [Repay](/guides/margin/repay).
