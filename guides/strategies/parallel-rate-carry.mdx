description: "Illustrative testnet workflow and its accounting limits."
mode: "wide"
---

A SmartAccount can borrow XLM and BLUSDC in their respective Vanna markets and supply each to the matching Blend reserve. Compare rates, quantities, free balances, and debt separately for both legs.

The account shares collateral and health across both positions. It is not two isolated loans with separate liquidation protection. Debt allocation shown by recovered position cards is derived and may not reconstruct the original strategy history.

Opening and exiting can require multiple transactions. If one leg fails after another confirms, inspect the account before retrying. Withdraw each supplied asset and repay its exact debt market on exit.
