Margin lets you borrow assets against collateral you deposit into a dedicated Margin Account — a smart contract that belongs to you. You create a per-user Margin Account, deposit collateral, and borrow assets against it, while Vanna's risk engine monitors your Health Factor continuously.

## How it works

A Margin Account is a per-user smart contract. You own it, and Vanna's risk engine monitors it to ensure your debt stays properly collateralized at all times.

You deposit collateral — XLM, USDC, or EURC — and the protocol calculates how much you can borrow based on the current market value of that collateral.

Your Health Factor — collateral value divided by debt value — must stay above 1.1× at all times. If it drops to 1.1×, your account becomes eligible for liquidation, meaning a liquidator can repay part of your debt and claim a portion of your collateral.

Borrowed assets stay inside your Margin Account. You can deploy them to Farm or Trade from within the account, but they cannot be sent directly to your regular wallet.

There is one Margin Account per user. All your collateral and debt live in that single account, regardless of how many different assets you deposit or borrow.

## Before you start

- **Wallet connected** — see [Connect Wallet](/guides/connect-wallet) if you haven't set this up yet.
- **Assets to deposit** — you'll need collateral (XLM, USDC, or EURC) ready in your wallet before you can borrow.

## Key metrics on the Margin tab

Once your Margin Account is created, the Margin tab displays a metrics bar with the following values.

| Metric | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Health Factor** | Collateral value divided by debt value. Must stay above 1.1× to avoid liquidation. |
| **Net Available Collateral** | How much more you can borrow before your Health Factor reaches 1.1×. |
| **Collateral Left Before Liquidation** | How much your collateral value can fall before your account becomes eligible for liquidation. |
