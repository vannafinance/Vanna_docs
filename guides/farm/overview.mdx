Farm deploys SmartAccount-held assets into external strategies.

| Type | Current integration |
|---|---|
| Single-asset lending | Blend XLM and Blend USDC supply |
| AMM liquidity | Soroswap and Aquarius pool interfaces |

Earn supplies Vanna's lending pools from your wallet and issues vTokens. Farm uses margin-account custody and external positions, which remain part of that account's risk assessment.

## Before you begin

Connect to the intended account and ensure it holds free assets matching the selected pool. BLUSDC, AqUSDC, and SoUSDC are not interchangeable. A displayed pool card does not guarantee controller registration, token permissions, or external pool availability. The Aquarius configuration records a smart-contract trustline/gauge limitation for its referenced pool.

Deployed positions can contribute recognized collateral, but their underlying is not free balance until exited. All positions in the SmartAccount share its health and debt.

See [Blend Pools](/guides/farm/blend-pools), [LP Pools](/guides/farm/amm-liquidity), and [Lite Mode](/guides/farm/leveraged-yield).
