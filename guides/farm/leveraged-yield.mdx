Lite Mode provides a simplified strategy interface and a view of current positions. It uses the same SmartAccount, collateral, and debt as Pro mode.

## Open a strategy

1. Switch to Lite Mode and select a supported strategy/pool.
2. Choose collateral, amount, and leverage inputs.
3. Review the deposit, borrowing, deployment amounts, rates, and health preview.
4. Confirm and complete each required signing step.
5. Wait for all steps to finish and check the resulting position and account debt.

Same-asset Blend strategies can try one atomic deposit/borrow/deploy transaction. The app can fall back to separate deposit/borrow and supply transactions. LP and cross-asset paths can require additional borrows, swaps, and liquidity operations. A no-borrow strategy uses standalone deposit rather than a zero-amount borrow.

**One-click does not guarantee one transaction.** If a later step fails, earlier confirmed steps remain. Inspect free balances and debt before retrying.

## Position recovery and exit

Positions are reconstructed from chain-backed Blend/LP balances and SmartAccount debt. Recovered leverage and opening/cost-basis metadata may be estimates rather than original trade history. Multiple positions share account debt.

Closing a Lite position withdraws/removes liquidity and repays applicable debt with available tokens. This can require several transactions and can leave residual assets or debt. It is separate from closing the SmartAccount contract. Check account-wide state after the exit.

Use Pro for advanced controls hidden by Lite mode. See [Farm](/guides/farm/overview) and [Repay](/guides/margin/repay).
