Vanna gives traders access to undercollateralized leverage without locking up excess capital. Whether you're new to leverage or an experienced DeFi trader, Vanna offers two ways to participate — switch between them anytime using the **Pro / Lite** toggle in the top navigation.

## Lite Mode

Lite Mode is Vanna's simplified one-click leveraged yield strategy. Select a pool, deposit collateral, set your leverage, and deploy — all in a single transaction. No manual collateral management, no separate borrow step.

</Card>

## Pro Mode

Pro Mode gives you full access to Vanna's Margin Account system — complete control over collateral, borrowing, and capital deployment across DeFi markets.

- **Open a Margin Account** — your own isolated smart contract for trading
- **Deposit collateral** — XLM, USDC, or EURC
- **Borrow up to 10× leverage** — undercollateralized, against your deposited collateral
- **Deploy composably** — use borrowed funds across spot swaps, yield farming, AMM pools — all from within one account
- **Monitor your Health Factor** — real-time risk tracking so you stay in control
- **Access Trade and Farm** — full access to spot trading and farming strategies

Pro Mode is for traders who want full flexibility: multi-asset collateral, composable strategies, and precise control over every position.
