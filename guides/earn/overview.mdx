Earn is Vanna's native lending pool system. You supply assets to a lending pool, receive vTokens in return, and earn yield from the interest paid by traders — passively, without managing any open positions.

## How lending pools work in Vanna

Vanna's lending pools are the core of the protocol's liquidity infrastructure. Liquidity providers deposit assets into these pools, making them available for traders who want to open leveraged positions. Traders borrow from the pools to fund their strategies — across spot, perpetuals, and yield farming markets — and pay interest on those borrows continuously.

That interest is added directly back into the pool, which means each vToken becomes redeemable for slightly more of the underlying asset over time. When you withdraw, you redeem your vTokens at the current exchange rate and receive more than you originally deposited — that difference is your earned yield.

The Supply APY adjusts dynamically based on borrower demand and pool utilization, so it moves as market conditions change.

## Key factors driving LP earnings

**1. Higher utilization from undercollateralized leverage** — Because Vanna allows traders to borrow more than typical overcollateralized protocols (up to 10×), the demand for borrowing is structurally higher. This keeps pool utilization elevated — and higher utilization means higher interest rates, which directly translates to higher yields for you as a supplier.

**2. Liquidation penalties shared with LPs** — When a trader's health factor drops below the threshold, their position is liquidated. A portion of the liquidation penalty is distributed to the lending pool, adding an additional revenue stream on top of borrower interest. This makes Vanna's yields more competitive than standard DeFi money markets.

**3. Dynamic interest rates** — Interest rates adjust automatically based on pool utilization. When more liquidity is borrowed, rates rise — rewarding you for providing capital when demand is high. When utilization drops, rates moderate — making borrowing cheaper and attracting more traders, which brings utilization back up.

## Before you start

- **Wallet connected** — your wallet must be connected to Vanna. See [Connect Your Wallet](/guides/connect-wallet) if you haven't done this yet.
- **Assets to supply** — the asset you want to supply, available in your connected wallet.

<Note>
  Earn positions carry smart contract risk and liquidity risk. If utilization is near 100%, withdrawals may be temporarily limited until borrowers repay. This is rare but possible.
</Note>
