The Stellar application supports spot swaps through Soroswap and Aquarius. Perps and options routes redirect to Spot.

## Choose the funding account

Use the available wallet or margin route and verify where the input tokens are held. Wallet swaps use wallet-held assets and may require issued-token trustlines. Margin swaps use SmartAccount-held assets through the protocol's approved execution path.

Soroswap USDC is **SoUSDC**; Aquarius USDC is **AqUSDC**. A generic USDC label inside a protocol-specific form does not mean Blend USDC is accepted.

## Steps

1. Open Spot and select the protocol, input/output assets, and funding account.
2. Enter the input quantity and obtain a current quote.
3. Review the output estimate, minimum output/slippage, balances, and destination.
4. Confirm/sign and wait for transaction confirmation.
5. Check actual input/output balances and, for margin swaps, account health.

Quotes can change before execution. Margin accounting can credit less collateral than the raw output received because controllers cap credit by accounted input and oracle value. External pool availability and account permissions can also reject the operation.

A spot swap does not open a perpetual contract or create an independent isolated margin position. See [Health Factor](/guides/margin/health-factor).
