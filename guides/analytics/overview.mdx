Analytics provides account and protocol monitoring plus scenario exploration. Use the overview, positions, liquidations, and risk-explorer sections to inspect the data available to the application.

## Data sources differ

Positions and overview use Stellar account/pool readers and derived metrics. Indexed activity can lag. Some oracle/alert views fall back to fixtures, and some scenario pages use mock distributions. Read the displayed source/status labels; not every chart is a live measurement.

## Health interpretation

Health factor at or below 1.1 makes a priced debt-bearing account a liquidation candidate. Color bands are display categories, not additional contract thresholds. HF below 1 describes collateral below debt; it does not prove a finalized accounting write-off.

The separate `/stats` surface uses Hubble mainnet data and is server-gated. It is not the testnet risk dashboard's authoritative balance source.
