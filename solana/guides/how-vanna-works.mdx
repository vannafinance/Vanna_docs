Vanna has two sides: **lenders** who supply assets to pools, and **margin accounts** that borrow from those pools and put the loan to work on other Solana protocols.

## The pieces

| Piece | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Lending pools** | One pool per lendable asset (SOL, USDC, USDT). Lenders deposit and receive vTokens (vSOL, vUSDC, vUSDT) that grow as interest accrues. |
| **Margin account** | A program-owned account per wallet. It holds your collateral, the tokens you borrowed and every receipt or position you open. |
| **Venues** | The protocols a margin account can act on: Jupiter and Titan (swaps), Phoenix (perps), Kamino (lending), Perena and Vanna's basis vaults (yield). |
| **Oracle** | Prices for every asset, from Kamino Scope first and Pyth as fallback. The app shows each source and its freshness on the Margin page. |
| **Health check** | Run on-chain after any action that could reduce health. The account must stay above a health factor of 1.10. |

## Follow the money

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lenders supply">
    A lender supplies USDC on Earn and receives vUSDC. The USDC sits in the pool, ready to be borrowed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A trader deposits collateral">
    A trader opens a margin account and deposits collateral, for example SOL, USDC or TSLAx.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The trader borrows">
    The trader borrows USDC from the pool. The USDC moves into the trader's margin account, not their wallet, and the account records the debt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The loan goes to work">
    From the margin account the trader swaps, opens a perp, or moves into a Farm vault. Each result (tokens, kSOL, vbSOL, a Phoenix position) stays in the account and counts as collateral.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Interest flows back">
    Borrowers pay interest that accrues to the pool. Lenders' vTokens are worth more underlying over time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unwind">
    The trader closes positions and repays (or uses Flash repay to do it all in one flow), then withdraws what's left.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Account health

```text
Health factor = total collateral value (USD) ÷ total debt value (USD)
```

Every asset counts at its full oracle value, with no per-asset weighting. At **1.10 or below** the account can be liquidated. The app blocks withdrawals and new borrows that would take you under a **1.15** safety floor, so you always keep a buffer. More in [Health Factor](/solana/guides/margin/health-factor).

## Predict

Predict is a separate flow on World.xyz markets. When you hold matching **Yes and No** shares of the same market, Vanna pairs them, merges them back into USDC and lends that USDC in its pool, so the pair earns interest until the event resolves. See [Earn While You Wait](/solana/guides/predict/earn-while-you-wait).
