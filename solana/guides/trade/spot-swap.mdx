**Trade → Spot** swaps tokens **inside your margin account**. It's how you turn borrowed USDC into SOL (a leveraged long), SOL into USDC (de-risking), or move between any supported tokens without the funds ever leaving the account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the aggregator">
    The card header reads **Swap via Jupiter**. Open the dropdown to switch to **Titan**. Jupiter is the default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what you pay">
    Under **You Pay**, pick the token and amount, or use **25% / 50% / 75% / Max**. **Swappable** is what your margin account holds of that token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what you receive">
    Under **You Receive**, pick the token. The quote fills in the amount you get.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the details">
    The rate line shows the price and your health factor before → after. Expand it for **Price Impact**, **Slippage Tolerance** and **Min. Received**. Slippage is **Auto (0.5%)** by default; change it with the gear icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the route">
    The **Route** card shows the hops and pools (for example *1 hop · Meteora DLMM*) and a badge for the aggregator that built it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap">
    Click **Swap** and approve. The swap runs on-chain from your margin account and is health-checked in the same transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Jupiter or Titan

You choose the aggregator; only that one is quoted, and the swap runs through it.

| Aggregator | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Jupiter** (default) | Jupiter's best route across Solana AMMs |
| **Titan** | Titan's meta-aggregated route. If Titan isn't available on the server or finds no route, the card says so; switch back to Jupiter |

Either way the swap executes from your margin account through Vanna's program, which only allows the routes and settings its validator approves and checks the account's health afterwards.

## The Margin account block

Beside the swap card: your health factor before → after with its bar, **Account leverage** before → after, **Borrowed**, and the **Swappable** balance of the token you're paying before → after. **Position History** below lists your past swaps.

## Things to know

- Swapping doesn't change your debt. It changes what your collateral is made of, so your health factor barely moves unless prices move afterwards.
- A swap that would leave the account below the safety floor is blocked before you sign.
- Without a wallet connected, only the swap card is shown.
