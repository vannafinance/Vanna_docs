**Farm** deploys tokens from your margin account into yield venues. You can farm with your own collateral or with borrowed funds (leveraged yield). Whatever you receive back, a Kamino receipt or vault shares, **stays in your margin account and counts as collateral**, so farming barely moves your health factor.

## Stats

| Stat | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Your Deposit TVL** | What you currently have deployed in Farm |
| **Your Earnings** | Yield earned across your Farm positions |
| **Net Farm APY** | Your blended APY across positions |
| **Pending Rewards** | Rewards not yet claimed |
| **Net Health Factor** | Your margin account's health factor (same number as on Margin) |

## Every vault page looks the same

Click a row to open its page:

- **Stats bar** on top (APY, rates, TVL…)
- **All Transactions**: your position value, receipt balance and earned yield, with Current Position and Position History tables
- **Analytics**: charts and details for that venue
- **Deposit / Withdraw panel** on the right. After you enter an amount, a details box shows the network, the amount, the **estimated receipt** you'll get, the share price, the APY and **projected monthly earnings**

## Why the health factor barely moves

A farm deposit swaps one thing in your margin account (USDC, SOL…) for another of about the same value (kUSDC, USD\*, vbSOL…), so total collateral stays the same. What can move it later is the receipt's own value: a vault's share price can rise or fall.
