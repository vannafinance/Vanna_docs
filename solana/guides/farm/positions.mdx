There are three places to see your Farm positions.

## Farm → Positions

On the Farm page, switch from **Vaults** to **Positions** to list only the vaults you hold, across Kamino, Perena and the basis vaults, with your holding in each.

## The vault page

Each vault page's **All Transactions** tab shows:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Position value** | What your holding is worth now, in USD |
| **Receipt balance** | Your kSOL / USD\* / vbBTC… |
| **Earned yield** | Your gain since your first deposit (the date is shown below it) |
| **Current Position** | Asset, protocol, supplied amount or value, receipt balance and APY |
| **Position History** | Every deposit and withdrawal with date, amount, status and transaction hash |

Use **Customize** to choose the columns and the filter to narrow the history.

## Margin → Positions

Farm receipts are collateral, so they also appear on the left of the Margin page's positions map. The **Farm** chip under **Borrowed money is in** shows how much is deployed in Farm and jumps there. Portfolio → Trader → **Farm** lists the same positions.

## Exiting

| Venue | How to exit |
|---|---|
| Kamino | **Withdraw** a percentage on the vault page; the token returns to your margin account |
| Perena | **Withdraw** shares; USDC returns to your margin account (USD\* Junior: 1% instant-exit fee) |
| Basis vaults | **Withdraw** shares; the vault unwinds both legs and returns USDC |

To close everything at once, including repaying your loans, use [Flash repay](/solana/guides/margin/repay#flash-repay). It redeems Kamino receipts automatically; vault shares can be withdrawn on their pages.
