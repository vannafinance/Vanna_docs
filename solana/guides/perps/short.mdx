A **short** profits when the price falls and loses when it rises. Losses on a short have no fixed ceiling, because a price can keep rising: watch your health factor.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the market">
    On **Trade → Perps**, choose SOL, ETH, BTC, TSLA or META and check the market is Open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Short">
    In the order panel click **Short**, with **Market** selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set collateral and leverage">
    Enter the USDC **Collateral** (or use MAX / the slider) and choose **Order leverage**. **Position size** updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the order">
    **Order on Phoenix** shows the estimated entry price and the **min entry price** your slippage allows, plus the account's health factor after the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    Click **Short** and approve. Collateral moves to Phoenix and the order fills in the same transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Funding on shorts

When funding is **positive** (most of the time in a rising market) shorts are paid every hour. When it turns **negative**, shorts pay. This is the same funding Vanna's [delta-neutral vaults](/solana/guides/farm/delta-neutral) earn by holding spot and an equal short.

## Closing

Click **Close** on the position (all or a percentage, as a reduce-only market order) or **Close All**. When nothing is left open, the USDC goes back to your margin account. [Flash repay](/solana/guides/margin/repay#flash-repay) also closes every perp position as its first step.
