A **long** profits when the price rises and loses when it falls.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the market">
    On **Trade → Perps**, choose the market from the selector at the top left (SOL, ETH, BTC, TSLA or META). Check **Market status** is Open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Long">
    In the order panel, keep **Long** selected and **Market**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set collateral">
    Enter the USDC **Collateral** for this order, use **MAX** or the 0–100% slider. **Available** is the USDC in your margin account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set leverage">
    Choose **Order leverage** (1× up to the market's maximum). **Position size** shows the size in the asset or in USD.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the order">
    **Order on Phoenix** shows the estimated entry price, the **max entry price** your slippage allows, and the account's health factor after the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    Click **Long** and approve. The USDC moves to Phoenix and the order fills in the same transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing the position

Your position appears in the positions table below the chart with market, side, size, collateral, leverage, entry and mark price, liquidation price and PnL. Click **Close** to close all or a percentage of it (a reduce-only market order); **Close All** closes every position. A **History** tab lists past orders. When nothing is left open in that account, the USDC returns to your margin account in a follow-up transaction.

## What moves your P&L

- The perp's price relative to your entry price
- Hourly funding: when it's positive, longs pay shorts
- Fees and slippage on open and close

All of it shows up in your margin account's health factor.
