**Analytics** is a live, public view of the whole protocol. It reads every margin account and pool directly from the chain. Nothing here sends a transaction.

The status bar at the top shows that the data is live, how many accounts were found, since when activity is counted, the last update time and a **Refresh** button.

## Sections

| Group | Page | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **Risk** | **Overview** | Is anything wrong? Bad debt, liquidatable accounts and debt, accounts at risk, average health and leverage, total debt |
| | **Positions** | Look up any wallet or margin account; how accounts spread across health, leverage and collateral |
| | **Liquidations** | Past liquidations |
| | **Top accounts** | The largest accounts |
| | **Alerts** | Accounts and pools that need attention |
| **Markets** | **Lending Pools** | Supplied, borrowed, available, utilization and rates per pool |
| | **Collateral** | What collateral is made of |
| | **Prices** | Oracle prices in use |
| **Prediction** | **Prediction Vaults** | Every Predict pool: YES / NO it holds and how much went into Vanna's USDC pool |
| **Tools** | **What-if Calculator** | How a price move would change accounts' health |

## Overview

The cards answer the key questions at a glance:

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Bad debt (estimated)** | Debt not covered by collateral. $0 means no account owes more than it holds |
| **Can be liquidated** | Accounts at a health factor of 1.10 or below, and their debt |
| **Liquidatable debt** | That debt, and the collateral backing it |
| **Accounts at risk / active** | Accounts close to the line, out of all active accounts |
| **Average health / leverage** | Across accounts with debt |
| **Collateral at risk** | Collateral in accounts with health under 1.50 |
| **Total debt** | Everything owed, against all collateral |

Below, the **Accounts** table lists every account **By health** or **By leverage**, filterable by Liquidatable, At risk, Safe, No debt and Not valued, with search by wallet or margin address. If you're connected, your own account is shown first.

Further down, **Accounts by health** charts debt at each health level (click a bar to filter the table), **What collateral is made of** splits all collateral into tokens held, Kamino deposits and Phoenix perps, and four cards sum up lending. **Test a price drop** opens the What-if Calculator.

## Positions

Paste any wallet or margin-account address into **Look up a wallet** and press **Look up** to open that account's own page (read-only). Below it:

- **Accounts by health**: how many accounts sit at each health level.
- **Debt by leverage**: how leveraged the borrowing accounts are.
- **Collateral by asset** and **Debt by asset**: what accounts hold and what they owe, per token.

## Prediction Vaults

<Frame caption="Prediction Vaults: how much Predict money is in Vanna's USDC pool, what is waiting, and one row per pool. ">
  <img src="/images/solana/analytics-prediction-vaults.png" alt="Prediction Vaults page with In Vanna's USDC pool, Waiting to merge, YES and NO shares in pools and Pools cards, a Where prediction money sits bar and a pools table with Earning statuses" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</Frame>

Every Predict market has a pool that holds users' YES and NO shares. Matched pairs are merged and supplied to Vanna's USDC pool (see [Earn While You Wait](/solana/guides/predict/earn-while-you-wait)). This page shows where that money is:

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **In Vanna's USDC pool** | USDC the Predict pools have supplied, and its share of the USDC pool |
| **Waiting to merge** | Matched YES + NO pairs not merged yet ($1 each) |
| **YES / NO shares in pools** | All shares held, and how many have nothing to pair with yet |
| **Pools** | Pools created, and how many hold funds |

The **Pools** table lists each market with YES and NO in its pool, matched pairs, USDC in Vanna's pool and a status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Earning** | Its pairs are in Vanna's USDC pool |
| **Merge pending** | Pairs are waiting for the next merge |
| **Waiting for other side** | Only YES or only NO so far: nothing to pair |
| **Event ended** | World closed the event before its pairs were merged |
| **Empty** | Nothing deposited yet |

Use the filters (With funds, Earning, Merge pending, Event ended, All pools) to narrow the table, and click a market to open it on Predict.

<Tip>
Use Analytics to sanity-check the protocol before you supply: how much is borrowed, how healthy borrowers are, and whether any bad debt exists.
</Tip>
