Test funds live in the **Deposit** window. Click **Deposit** in the header (next to your account) to open **Portfolio account**, then use **Test funds** on the **Deposit** tab. You can also open it from the **Deposit** button on [Portfolio](/solana/guides/portfolio).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    [Connect](/solana/guides/connect-wallet) with email, a social login or Backpack.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Deposit">
    Click **Deposit** in the header. The window shows your wallet and the **Testnet** tag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mint">
    Click **Mint** on a token, or **Mint all** for every token at once. Each tile shows what was added and your new balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Token | Per mint |
|---|---|
| SOL, USDC, USDT | +1,000 |
| JitoSOL, JupSOL, JupUSD | +1,000 |
| NVDAx, GOOGLx, AAPLx, TSLAx | +10 |
| CASH (Predict) | +1,000 |

Minting is **unlimited on the fork**: mint again whenever you run low.

## How it works

Test funds use Surfpool **fork cheatcodes**: the server writes the new balance straight into fork state. There is no transaction to sign and no wallet approval. Nothing real moves; these tokens exist only on the fork.

## The rest of the Deposit window

| Part | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Receive** | Your wallet address with a QR code and **Copy address**, to receive fork tokens from another Vanna tester. Only Vanna testnet tokens show up here; devnet or mainnet tokens won't |
| **Transfer** tab | Move tokens between your wallet (**Portfolio account**) and your **Margin account**, the same form as [Transfer Collateral](/solana/guides/margin/transfer-collateral) |

<Tip>
- Keep some SOL in your wallet for transaction fees.
- CASH is the token World.xyz markets settle in. You only need it for [Predict](/solana/guides/predict/overview).
- Using Backpack and a balance doesn't appear there? Check Backpack is pointed at the [fork RPC](/solana/guides/connect-wallet#add-the-vanna-rpc-to-your-wallet). The app itself always reads the fork.
</Tip>
