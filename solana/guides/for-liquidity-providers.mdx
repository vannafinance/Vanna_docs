## What you earn

You supply an asset to its lending pool and receive **vTokens** (vSOL, vUSDC, vUSDT). Borrowers pay interest into the pool, so each vToken is redeemable for more of the underlying over time. Your **Supply APY** depends on how much of the pool is borrowed (its utilization): the busier the pool, the higher the rate. See [Interest Rate Model](/solana/learn/interest-rate-model).

## What you risk

- **Liquidity:** you can withdraw only what isn't currently lent out. In a very busy pool (high utilization) you may have to wait for repayments or liquidations to free cash.
- **Bad debt:** if a borrower's collateral falls faster than it can be liquidated, the pool can take a loss. Vanna liquidates at a health factor of 1.10, while every borrower still has more collateral than debt, to keep this rare. The [Analytics](/solana/guides/analytics) page shows estimated bad debt and accounts at risk live.
- **Test network:** today's app runs on a mainnet fork; balances are test balances.

## Other ways to earn

- **Predict:** hold matched Yes and No shares and they earn in Vanna's USDC pool until the event resolves ([Predict](/solana/guides/predict/earn-while-you-wait)).
- **Farm:** through a margin account you can supply to Kamino or deposit into Perena and basis vaults ([Farm](/solana/guides/farm/overview)).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and get test funds">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Supply">
    Open [Earn](/solana/guides/earn/overview), pick a pool and [supply](/solana/guides/earn/supply).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track it">
    Watch interest on [Portfolio → Lender](/solana/guides/portfolio).
  </Step>
</Steps>
