The **Margin** page (also the page the app opens on) is where you manage your margin account: deposit collateral, borrow, repay and move collateral in or out.

## Account stats

| Stat | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Net Health Factor** | Collateral ÷ debt. Liquidatable at 1.10 or below. **No debt** when you owe nothing |
| **Collateral Left Before Liquidation** | How much value your collateral can lose before the account hits 1.10 |
| **Net Available Collateral** | Collateral minus debt: your equity |
| **Net amount Borrowed** | Total debt in USD, including accrued interest |
| **Net Leverage Taken** | Everything the account holds (borrowed funds included, open perps at full size) ÷ your equity. 1.00x = no borrowing |

## The three tabs

| Tab | What it does | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Leverage your Assets** | Deposit collateral and borrow in one transaction, with a 1×–10× leverage slider | [Deposit](/solana/guides/margin/deposit-collateral) · [Borrow](/solana/guides/margin/borrow) |
| **Repay Loan** | Repay from the tokens in your margin account; Flash repay closes everything | [Repay](/solana/guides/margin/repay) |
| **Transfer Collateral** | Move tokens between your wallet (Portfolio account) and your margin account | [Transfer Collateral](/solana/guides/margin/transfer-collateral) |

## Margin Account Info

The right column shows Total Borrowed value, Net Available Collateral, Total Value, Net Health Factor and Net Borrow Rate, then:

- **Liquidation**: your health factor on a bar, the 1.10 liquidation line and the dollar buffer left before it.
- **Oracles and LTs**: the oracle program, the 1.10× liquidation threshold, the Vanna program address, and every token's live price with its source (Kamino Scope or Pyth) and freshness.

## Positions

<Frame caption="Current Positions: collateral on the left, the account value in the middle, each loan on the right, and where the borrowed money is.">
  <img src="/images/solana/margin/positions.png" alt="Account map with five collaterals (SOL, AAPLx, GOOGLx, USDC and more behind Show all) linked to an Account value node with interest accrued and borrow rate, two loans (SOL 90% of debt, USDC 10% of debt) with Repay buttons, Flash repay and share buttons, and Borrowed money is in chips for Spot, Farm and Idle" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</Frame>

**Current Positions** draws the whole account as one map:

- **Collateral** on the left: every token the account holds, including Farm receipts such as kSOL, kUSDC, USD\* and vbSOL. **Show all** expands a long list.
- **Account value** in the middle, with **Interest accrued** and the **Borrow rate**.
- **Borrowed** on the right: each loan with its share of total debt and a **Repay** button.
- **Borrowed money is in**: chips showing how much of the loan sits in **Spot**, **Perps** or **Farm** positions and how much is **Idle** in the account. Click a chip to jump to that page.
- **Flash repay** unwinds the account; the share button makes a shareable card.

**Positions History** lists past actions.
