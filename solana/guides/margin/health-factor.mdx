description: "The one number that decides whether your margin account is safe."

```text
Health factor = total collateral value (USD) ÷ total debt value (USD)
```

- **Collateral** is everything in your margin account at its oracle price: tokens, borrowed tokens still in the account, Kamino receipts, vault shares and your Phoenix perps collateral (with open P&L).
- **Debt** is every loan, with interest accrued to now.
- Every asset counts at **full value**: there are no per-asset collateral weights.

With no debt the health factor is shown as **No debt**. Very large values show as **>100**.

## Zones

| Health factor | Label | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1.50 or more | **Safe** | Comfortable buffer |
| 1.25 – 1.50 | **Healthy** | Fine, but a sharp move would bring you closer |
| Above 1.10, below 1.25 | **At risk** | Orders that would take you between 1.15 and 1.25 show a warning; below 1.15 they're blocked |
| **1.10 or below** | **Liquidation** | Anyone can liquidate the account |

The app keeps a **1.15 safety floor**: it won't build a borrow, withdrawal or trade that would leave you below it. The program's hard rule is 1.10: it rejects any action that would leave the account at or below that.

## Example

You deposit $10,000 of SOL and borrow $5,000 USDC, which stays in the account.

- Collateral = $10,000 SOL + $5,000 USDC = $15,000; debt = $5,000 → **HF 3.00**
- You swap the $5,000 USDC into SOL: collateral is still $15,000 (now all SOL) → **HF 3.00**
- SOL falls 60%: collateral = $6,000 → **HF 1.20**, close to the line
- SOL falls 64%: collateral ≈ $5,400 → **HF 1.08**, liquidatable

Moving value between assets inside the account (a swap, a Kamino supply, a vault deposit) barely changes the health factor, because what goes out comes back at about the same value. What moves it is **prices** and **debt**.

## Where you see it

Portfolio, Margin (stats, Account Info and the Liquidation bar), Spot, Perps and Farm all show your health factor, and every action shows it **before → after** before you sign.

## Raising it

- Deposit more collateral
- Repay some debt
- Close or reduce risky positions
