There are two ways to deposit:

| Where | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Leverage your Assets** tab | You want to deposit and borrow in one transaction |
| **Transfer Collateral** (Portfolio account → Margin account) | You only want to move tokens into the margin account |

## Deposit (and borrow) in one go

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Leverage your Assets">
    On **Margin**, keep the first tab selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the token and source">
    Under **Deposit**, choose the token. **WB** deposits from your wallet balance; **MB** borrows against collateral already in your margin account (one token, borrow only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Type it or use **10% / 25% / 50% / 100%**. The USD value and current price are shown under the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add more collateral (optional)">
    **+ Add Collateral** adds another token to the same transaction, so you can deposit several assets at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Borrow, or not">
    Set the borrow amount or leverage (see [Borrow](/solana/guides/margin/borrow)). To deposit only, leave the borrow at zero.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and sign">
    Check **Transaction Details** and the before → after account preview, then click **Deposit & Borrow** (or deposit) and approve.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Accepted collateral

SOL, USDC, JitoSOL, JupSOL, JupUSD, NVDAx, GOOGLx, AAPLx and TSLAx can be deposited. USDT can be held, borrowed, repaid and withdrawn, but isn't offered as a deposit.

Each asset counts at its full oracle value: health factor is total collateral ÷ total debt with no per-asset haircut.

<Note>
When you deposit SOL from your wallet, the app keeps a small SOL reserve (0.05 SOL) back so you can still pay transaction fees.
</Note>
