Your margin account is a program-owned account derived from your wallet address. You create it once; after that it's always the same account for that wallet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    [Connect your wallet](/solana/guides/connect-wallet) and mint test funds from **Deposit** ([Faucet](/solana/guides/faucet)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Margin">
    Go to **Margin** (the page the app opens on). If you don't have an account yet, the page offers to create one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and sign">
    Click create and approve in your wallet. A small amount of SOL pays the account's rent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
You can also skip this step: your first deposit (in **Leverage your Assets** or **Transfer Collateral** into the margin account) creates the account in the same transaction if it doesn't exist yet.
</Tip>

## What the account holds

- Collateral you deposit
- Tokens you borrow (they stay in the account; they don't go to your wallet)
- Swap results, Kamino receipts (kSOL, kUSDC, kUSDT), vault shares (USD\*, USD\* Junior, vbSOL, vbBTC…) and your Phoenix perps collateral

All of it counts toward one health factor.
