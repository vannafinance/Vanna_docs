<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pool">
    On [Earn](/solana/guides/earn/overview), click the pool you want (SOL, USDC or USDT).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Supply Liquidity">
    In the right-hand panel keep **Supply Liquidity** selected and check the token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    Type an amount or use **10% / 25% / 50% / 100%** of your wallet balance (shown as **Bal**). For SOL a small amount is kept back for fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check More Details">
    Expand **More Details** to see what you'll receive (**You Get vSOL**), the current exchange rate (**SOL per vSOL**), the current Supply APY, estimated monthly and yearly earnings at that APY, and the **Remaining Supply Cap**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supply and sign">
    Click **Supply Liquidity** and approve in your wallet. When the transaction confirms, your vTokens appear and the pool's **Your Supply** updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- Supply APY changes with utilization; the estimate uses today's rate, not a guarantee.
- You can supply more at any time; every deposit mints vTokens at the current exchange rate.
- Your earnings show on [Portfolio → Lender](/solana/guides/portfolio) as **Interest earned** and in the 30-day Lending P&L chart.
