**Earn** lists Vanna's lending pools. Supplying to a pool lends your tokens to margin accounts; their interest is what you earn.

## Pools

| Pool | Receipt | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SOL | vSOL | Borrowed for SOL longs and SOL-denominated strategies |
| USDC | vUSDC | Usually the busiest pool: most leverage, perps collateral and vaults run on USDC |
| USDT | vUSDT | Stablecoin pool |

## Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Assets Supplied** | Total supplied to the pool, in tokens and USD |
| **Supply APY** | What lenders currently earn |
| **Assets Borrowed** | What margin accounts currently owe the pool |
| **Borrow APY** | What borrowers currently pay |
| **Utilization Rate** | Borrowed ÷ supplied. Rates rise with utilization |
| **Your Supply** | Your share of the pool, in tokens and USD |

Use **Vaults / Positions** to switch between all pools and just yours, and **All Pools**, search, **Deposit** and **Supply APY is** to filter. The charts above the table show your **Overall Deposit** and **Net Earnings** over 1 week, 1 month, 3 months or all time.

## Pool page

Click a pool for its detail page: Total Supply, Available Liquidity, Utilization Rate and Supply APY on top, the **Supply Liquidity / Withdraw Liquidity** panel on the right, and these tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Your Positions** | Your supply in this pool over time (**My Supply** chart) |
| **Details** | Statistics: total supplied and borrowed, available liquidity, supply and borrow APY, utilization, liquidation bonus, oracle price and source, share-token exchange rate, reserve factor, supply and borrow caps, status and rate curve |
| **Activity** | The pool's split between idle and borrowed funds, and your own supplies and withdrawals in this pool |
| **Collateral and Limits** | The account-wide liquidation rule, each asset's collateral parameters and how much of each cap is used |
| **Analytics** | The rate curve; on the USDC pool, also how much USDC came from each [Predict market](/solana/guides/predict/earn-while-you-wait#where-to-see-it) |

## Your receipt: vTokens

Supplying mints vTokens to your wallet. The number of vTokens you hold stays the same; what each one is worth in the underlying asset (the exchange rate, e.g. "SOL per vSOL") rises as interest accrues. See [vTokens](/solana/learn/vtokens).
