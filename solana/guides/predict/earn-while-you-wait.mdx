Prediction shares usually sit idle for days or weeks until the event resolves. On Vanna they earn.

## Pairing

For any one market, **1 Yes share + 1 No share** always pays exactly **$1** at resolution, whatever happens. So a Yes/No pair is as good as $1 of USDC. Vanna uses that:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your shares go into Vanna's pool">
    When you buy on Predict, your shares are deposited into Vanna's outcome pool for that market in the same approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Yes and No are paired">
    The pool matches Yes shares against No shares held in it (from you and from other users).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pairs are merged into USDC and lent">
    Each pair is merged back into $1 of USDC and that USDC is supplied to Vanna's USDC lending pool, where it earns the pool's supply rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Yield comes back as extra shares">
    The interest is paid to you as extra shares of the side you hold. Because it's extra Yes (or No) shares, the yield pays out only if your side wins.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Shares that can't be paired yet (more Yes than No in the pool, or the reverse) are **waiting**: they're held safely but don't earn until the other side arrives.

## Reading the numbers

| Where | Field | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Event card | **Earn up to X%** | The best rate any side of this event can earn right now |
| Trade panel | **Paired** | How much of the shares you're buying will be paired |
| Trade panel | **Your paired Yes** | Your paired shares before → after this trade |
| Trade panel | **Est. yield** | Estimated earnings per month at the current rate |
| Earning tab | **Pairs / Yes earns / No earns** | Pool balance per market and what each side earns |
| Summary strip | **Earning in USDC pool / Passive yield / Earned** | Your totals across all markets |

The side that's scarcer in the pool earns more, because each of its shares backs more of the pool's pairs.

## Where to see it

The same numbers show up in two places:

- **Earn → USDC → Analytics** lists how much USDC each Predict market has supplied to the pool.
- **Analytics → Prediction Vaults** shows every Predict pool: shares held, pairs waiting and USDC supplied. See [Analytics](/solana/guides/analytics#prediction-vaults).

## Risks

- The yield is paid in shares of your side, so if your side loses, the yield is worth $0 along with it.
- The pool's USDC is lent through Vanna's USDC pool and carries that pool's risks (see [For Liquidity Providers](/solana/guides/for-liquidity-providers)).
- Rates move with the USDC pool's supply APY and with how balanced Yes and No are.
