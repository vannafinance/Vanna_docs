**Predict** brings World.xyz prediction markets into Vanna. Every market is a question with a **Yes** and a **No** share. A winning share pays **$1** when the event resolves; a losing share pays nothing. The price of a share (in cents) is roughly the market's probability for that outcome.

What Vanna adds: your shares don't just sit. Matched Yes and No shares are lent into Vanna's USDC pool, so your position **earns while you wait** for the result.

## The page

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Summary strip** | Predictions value, how much is Earning in the USDC pool, your Passive yield APY, what you've Earned, and what your positions **Pay if right** |
| **Linked wallets** | The wallets whose World positions Vanna tracks (your connected wallet; **Add wallet** is coming soon), and whether any World positions are waiting to earn |
| **Filters** | Trending, Crypto, Sports, Politics, Economics, All, plus search and **My positions** |
| **Event cards** | Each event's outcomes with live probabilities, the last trade, volume and the **Earn up to** rate for that event |

Trending follows World.xyz's own trending list.

## Prices

Prices come live from World's maker quotes, with recent trades as a fallback. Each outcome shows a Yes price and a No price; together they add up to a little over $1 (the maker's spread). An outcome with no quote and no recent trade isn't tradable until one appears.

## What you pay with

Predict trades settle in **CASH**, World's USD token. Mint test CASH from **Deposit → Test funds** ([Faucet](/solana/guides/faucet)).
