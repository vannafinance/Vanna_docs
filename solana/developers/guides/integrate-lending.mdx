---
title: "Integrate Lending"
description: "Supply to and redeem from a Vanna pool from another client."
mode: "wide"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Derive accounts">
    `asset_config`, `reserve`, `share_mint`, the reserve's liquidity vault, the user's underlying ATA, and the user's share ATA (classic SPL Token).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wrap SOL">
    If the mint is wSOL, wrap native SOL first. The program only sees wSOL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh and quote">
    Read the `Reserve`. If `last_update_timestamp` is old, include `public_refresh_reserve` or project accrual off-chain so the quote matches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supply">
    Call `lender_supply(assets, min_shares_out)` with `min_shares_out` from the quoted rate minus slippage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Redeem">
    Call `lender_redeem(shares, min_assets_out)`. Payouts are limited to `accounted_liquidity_assets − accrued_protocol_fees`, so check available cash first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Pools exist for USDC, USDT and SOL. Supply is subject to `supply_cap`, the operating mode, and the reserve status.

vTokens are **not** margin collateral. To use supplied funds as collateral, redeem first and deposit the underlying.

App reference: `lib/solana/lend.ts` (`supply`, `redeem`, `previewSupplyShares`).
