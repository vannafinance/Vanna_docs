Source: `programs/vanna_credit_layer/src/state/` and `programs/vanna_oracle/src/{price_book,market_book}.rs`.

## ProtocolConfig `["protocol"]`

`admin`, `pending_admin`, `treasury`, `operating_mode`, `max_assets_per_margin`, `next_asset_index`, `allowed_tokens[24]`, `max_debts_per_margin`, `max_venues_per_margin`.

`allowed_tokens` is the protocol-wide list of asset indexes that count as collateral. Every margin's health scan walks this list.

## AssetConfig `["asset", mint]` or `["asset", venue]`

`mint`, `token_program`, `reserve` (default if there is no pool), `max_collateral_per_margin`, `ltv_bps`, `liquidation_threshold_bps`, `liquidation_bonus_bps`, `asset_index`, `decimals`, `collateral_enabled`, `borrow_enabled`, `kind` (`Token` or `Venue`), `oracle`, `issuer_controls_accepted`.

`max_collateral_per_margin` caps how much of a token counts toward health. `u64::MAX` means uncapped, and `0` means it counts for nothing (a liquidator can still seize it).

## Reserve `["reserve", mint]`

`asset_config`, `underlying_mint`, `liquidity_vault`, `share_mint`, `supply_cap`, `borrow_cap`, `accounted_liquidity_assets`, `total_borrow_assets`, `total_borrow_shares`, `borrow_index_wad`, `accrued_protocol_fees`, `last_update_timestamp`, `rate_curve`, `reserve_factor_bps`, `status`, `total_supply_shares`, `interest_remainder_wad`, `fee_remainder`.

The liquidity vault is the ATA of the reserve PDA. The share mint is `["share_mint", mint]` under classic SPL Token, even when the underlying is Token-2022.

## MarginAccount `["margin", authority]`

`authority`, `status`, `venue_count`, `debt_count`, `venue_asset_indexes[24]`, `debt_asset_indexes[24]`, `event_sequence`.

There is no collateral ledger. A margin's collateral is the live balance of its vaults (`ATA(mint, margin)`) for every allowed token. The account itself tracks only its venues and debts.

## DebtPosition `["debt", margin, reserve]`

`margin_account`, `reserve`, `borrow_shares`. Token debt is derived from shares.

## Integration `["integration", program_id]`

`program_id`, `validator`, `enabled`.

## Oracle: PriceBook `["price_book"]`

Owned by `vanna_oracle`. Up to 32 `PriceSource { mint, decimals, config: OracleConfig }` entries.

`OracleConfig`: `scope_prices`, `scope_chain[4]`, `scope_twap_chain[4]`, `pyth_price`, `pyth_factor`, `receipt_account`, `receipt_program`, `max_age_secs`, `max_twap_divergence_bps`, `max_confidence_bps`.

## Oracle: MarketBook `["market_book", venue]`

Owned by `vanna_oracle`. `venue`, `program`, `collateral_mint`, `collateral_decimals`, `collateral_price` (an `OracleConfig`), and up to 32 `MarketEntry { market, asset_id, max_leverage_bps, trading_enabled, index_price }`.
