## Events

Liquidation, write-off and `margin_execute` events are emitted through a self-CPI (`emit_cpi!`), so they survive long external-program logs such as Phoenix's. Read them from the inner instructions. Other events (`Borrowed`, `DebtRepaid`, `CollateralDeposited`, `LiquiditySupplied`, and so on) are in `events.rs`.
