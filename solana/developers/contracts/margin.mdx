Source: `instructions/account_manager/{account,collateral}.rs`.

## `user_create_margin`

Initialize `["margin", authority]`. One per wallet. Authority = signer.

## `user_deposit_collateral(amount)`

`init_if_needed` the margin vault (`ATA(mint, margin)`), then a measured transfer in. The asset must be a collateral-enabled token, not a venue.

Any allowed token that reaches the vault counts, however it got there: deposit, borrow, swap, or a plain transfer.

## `user_withdraw_collateral(amount, min_health_factor_wad)`

Transfer from the margin vault to the wallet. If the margin has debt, the full-set health factor after the withdrawal must exceed 1.10 and `min_health_factor_wad`. A debt-free withdrawal reads no prices at all.

## `user_reclaim_rent`

Close an empty margin vault, a fully repaid `DebtPosition`, or both, and return the rent to the authority.

## `user_close_margin`

Requires no debts and no venues. Closes the margin PDA and returns its rent.

## Limits

Each margin holds at most `max_debts_per_margin` debts and `max_venues_per_margin` venues. Collateral tokens are bounded by the protocol's allowed-token list, so every health scan has a fixed upper cost.
