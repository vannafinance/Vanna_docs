Source: `instructions/account_manager/borrow.rs`.

## `user_borrow(assets, max_debt_shares)`

1. Gate on operating mode, reserve status (`Active`) and `borrow_enabled`.
2. `init_if_needed` the `DebtPosition ["debt", margin, reserve]` and the margin vault. The margin must have room under `max_debts_per_margin` for a new debt.
3. Accrue. Require `total_borrow + assets ≤ borrow_cap` and `assets ≤ available_cash`.
4. Mint debt shares (rounded up), at most `max_debt_shares`.
5. Transfer the tokens from the reserve to the margin vault.
6. Value every holding through the oracle. The full-set health factor must exceed 1.10.

Borrowed tokens land in the margin vault, so a borrow raises both collateral and debt. A Token-2022 borrow is health-checked on what actually arrives, net of transfer fees.

Only the pool assets (USDC, USDT, SOL) are `borrow_enabled`.

## `user_repay_from_margin(max_assets, repay_all)`

Repay from the margin vault's own balance. `repay_all` repays the exact accrued debt. The amount is capped at what the vault holds. When shares reach zero, the debt leaves the margin's active list. Close the `DebtPosition` with `user_reclaim_rent`.

<Note>
There is no wallet-side repay instruction for owners. To repay with wallet funds, deposit them into the margin first, then repay from margin. Liquidators repay from their own wallet inside `public_liquidate`.
</Note>
