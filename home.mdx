Vanna has two sides. **Lenders** supply assets to lending pools and earn interest. **Traders** open a margin account, deposit collateral, borrow up to 10x, and use the borrowed funds in swaps, farms and other protocols without leaving the account. One health factor covers the whole account.

Vanna runs on **Stellar** and **Solana**. Both use the same app at [test.vanna.finance](https://test.vanna.finance): pick your chain in the switcher at the top right.

| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Lending pools | XLM, BLUSDC, AqUSDC, SoUSDC | SOL, USDC, USDT |
| Margin account, up to 10x | Yes, with Single and Dual Borrow | Yes |
| Collateral | XLM, USDC, Blend and LP positions | SOL, LSTs, stables, tokenized stocks |
| Spot swaps | Soroswap, Aquarius | Jupiter, Titan |
| Perpetuals | — | Phoenix |
| Farming | Blend, Soroswap and Aquarius LP | Kamino, Perena, delta-neutral vaults |
| Prediction markets | — | World.xyz |
| Analytics and Copilot | Yes | Yes, plus an MCP connector |
| Network | Stellar testnet | Solana mainnet fork |
