<Note>
Vanna runs on a **Solana mainnet fork** (Surfpool), not public Devnet or Mainnet. Balances are test balances on the fork. If you use Backpack, add the fork RPC `https://rpc-devnet.solana.vanna.finance` to it ([how](/solana/guides/connect-wallet#add-the-vanna-rpc-to-your-wallet)). Then mint test funds from **Deposit → Test funds** ([Faucet](/solana/guides/faucet)) before any Earn, Margin, Trade, Farm or Predict action.
</Note>
