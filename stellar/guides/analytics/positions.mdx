**Analytics → Positions** answers two questions: what does a given account hold, and how are all accounts spread out?

## Look up an account

Paste a **wallet** (starts with G) or **margin account** (starts with C) address into **Look up a wallet**. The account page shows its health, a what-if slider on its own balances, how it ranks, what it holds and owes, and its recent on-chain events. An account outside the latest scan is read live from the chain.

## Distributions

| Chart | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Accounts by health** | How many accounts (and how much debt) sit in each health band |
| **Debt by leverage** | Debt per leverage band |
| **Collateral by asset** | What accounts hold, including Blend deposits (BLEND_XLM, BLEND_USDC) and LP positions (SS_XLM_USDC) |
| **Debt by asset** | What accounts owe, with each asset's share |
| **Positions by bucket** | The detailed monitor: health and leverage buckets, borrow rates, the health heatmap and the positions in each bucket |

## Good to know

- A wallet (G…) and its margin account (C…) are different addresses. Debt belongs to the margin account.
- An account with no debt shows **No debt** instead of a health factor.
- Health bands and heatmap colours are display choices. The only contract threshold is **1.10**.
- Click **Refresh** at the top before acting on an account close to the line.
