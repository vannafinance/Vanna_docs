**Analytics** is a public, protocol-wide risk dashboard. It reads the list of margin accounts straight from the **Registry** contract, then reads each account's collateral and debt and prices them with Vanna's Reflector-backed oracle. There's no private database: anyone can check every account. No wallet is needed to view it.

The strip at the top says what you're looking at, for example **Live · Stellar testnet · 200 open margin accounts read from the Registry · 4 lending pools · Reflector prices**, with the update time and a **Refresh** button. The deep scan covers the most recent 200 accounts.

## Sections

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Risk">
    | Section | What it answers |
    |---|---|
    | **Overview** | Is anything wrong? Bad debt, liquidatable accounts and debt, accounts at risk, average health and leverage, total debt and collateral, then the Accounts table and lending summary |
    | **Positions** | Look up any wallet; accounts by health and leverage; collateral and debt by asset |
    | **Liquidations** | Who can be liquidated now, who's closest, past liquidations and the 1.10 line |
    | **Top accounts** | Debt concentration, largest borrowers and recent activity |
    | **Alerts** | What needs attention now, by priority (Act now, Critical, Warning, For information) |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Markets">
    | Section | What it answers |
    |---|---|
    | **Lending Pools** | Supplied, borrowed and available to withdraw per pool, plus the on-chain borrow-rate curve |
    | **Collateral** | Which assets accounts hold, how each is priced, and the kind (token, Blend deposit, LP) |
    | **Prices** | The Reflector price for each asset and whether the latest read was live |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Tools">
    | Section | What it answers |
    |---|---|
    | **What-if Calculator** | Reprice every account with your own price moves and see before / after |
    | **Risk Explorer** | Stress scenarios run against the live accounts |
    | **Advanced positions** | Positions grouped by collateral type, correlated-drop groups and borrow-rate impact |
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

All tools are **read-only**: they never send a transaction or ask for a signature.

## Reading the numbers

- **Accounts table:** your own margin account is listed first, with its real rank. Click a row to open that account's page.
- **Health bands** are display buckets. The only contract threshold is **1.10**.
- **Bad debt (estimated)** is an account owing more than it holds. It's a derived figure, not a write-off in the pools.
- **Empty accounts** (no collateral and no debt) aren't counted as active.
