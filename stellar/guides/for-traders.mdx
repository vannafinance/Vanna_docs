A **margin account** is your own Soroban smart contract. It holds your collateral and everything you borrow, swap into or farm. One health factor covers all of it.

## Your path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    Open **Margin** and click **Create your Margin Account**. See [Open a Margin Account](/stellar/guides/margin/open-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit and borrow">
    In **Leverage your Assets**, deposit XLM, BLUSDC, AqUSDC or SoUSDC and borrow one asset (**Single Borrow**) or two (**Dual Borrow**) at 1x to 10x. See [Borrow](/stellar/guides/margin/borrow).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the funds to work">
    [Swap](/stellar/guides/trade/spot-swap) on Soroswap or Aquarius, or [Farm](/stellar/guides/farm/overview) on Blend, Soroswap and Aquarius, straight from the margin account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch your health factor">
    Keep it comfortably above **1.10**. The Margin page's **Liquidation** card shows how far you are from the line in dollars. See [Health Factor](/stellar/guides/margin/health-factor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unwind">
    Exit Farm positions, [repay](/stellar/guides/margin/repay) your loans, then [transfer collateral](/stellar/guides/margin/transfer-collateral) back to your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits to know

- New borrows must keep the account's health factor above **1.10**, and the pool must have cash to lend. A pool can't be borrowed past **95%** utilization.
- Spot blocks a swap that would drop your health factor below **1.15**.
- BLUSDC, AqUSDC and SoUSDC are different tokens. Borrow the one your venue uses.
