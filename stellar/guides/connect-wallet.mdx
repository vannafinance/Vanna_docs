## Supported sign-in methods

Click **Connect wallet** at the top right. The **Sign in to Vanna** dialog lists:

| Method | Status on Stellar testnet |
|---|---|
| **Freighter** | Live. Set Freighter's network to **Testnet**. |
| **OneKey** | Live. Unlock OneKey and make sure it has a Stellar account. |
| **LOBSTR** | Shown as **Soon**. LOBSTR signs for Stellar mainnet only. |
| **Email, Google, X, GitHub** | Shown only when email sign-in is enabled for the build. It is powered by Dynamic and gives you a Stellar wallet without an extension. |

<Note>
The dialog also lists Solana wallets. Picking one switches you to Vanna on Solana; Stellar positions stay with your Stellar address.
</Note>

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the wallet">
    Install [Freighter](https://www.freighter.app/), create or import an account, and switch its network to **Testnet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in dialog">
    Go to [test.vanna.finance](https://test.vanna.finance), enter your access code if asked, choose **Stellar** in the chain switcher at the top right, and click **Connect wallet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the connection">
    Pick **Freighter** (or OneKey) and approve in the wallet pop-up. Your wallet name, for example **@Freighter**, replaces the button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the address">
    Open the wallet menu to see your address. If you already have a margin account, Vanna finds it on-chain automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Get test tokens

Click **Deposit** in the header. The **Portfolio account** modal opens on the **Deposit** tab:

<Frame caption="Test funds with one-click Mint per token and Mint all, plus your receive address and QR code. Click the image to zoom.">
  <img src="/images/stellar/onboarding/deposit-modal-test-funds.png" alt="Portfolio account modal showing Freighter GCQP...ZUQ5 on Testnet, Deposit and Transfer tabs, Test funds tiles for XLM, BLUSDC, AqUSDC and SoUSDC with Mint buttons, a Mint all button, and a Receive panel with QR code and Copy address" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
</Frame>

| Token | Source | How often |
|---|---|---|
| **XLM** | Stellar Friendbot, 10,000 XLM | Once per account (Friendbot funds an address only once) |
| **BLUSDC** | Blend testnet faucet. Sends a basket: USDC, BLND, wETH and wBTC | Once per wallet |
| **AqUSDC** | Aquarius testnet faucet, 1,000 per mint | Any time |
| **SoUSDC** | Soroswap testnet faucet | Any time, with a short cooldown between mints |

**Mint all** mints every token that isn't already funded or cooling down. Some mints open your wallet once to add the token's trustline; approve it. The **Receive** panel shows your address and QR code. Send only Stellar **testnet** assets there; mainnet XLM or USDC will not show up in Vanna.

<Tip>
Keep some XLM in your wallet. Every Stellar transaction pays a small XLM fee, and each trustline raises your account's minimum XLM reserve. The app always leaves that reserve untouched when you deposit or supply XLM.
</Tip>

## Move funds to your margin account

The modal's **Transfer** tab moves tokens between your wallet and your margin account. It is the same form as **Margin → Transfer Collateral**. See [Transfer Collateral](/stellar/guides/margin/transfer-collateral).

## Reconnect

Use the same wallet to return to the same positions. A different address has different balances and a different margin account.
