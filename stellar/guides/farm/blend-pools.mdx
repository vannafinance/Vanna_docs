Blend supply moves **XLM** or **BLUSDC** from your margin account into Blend's testnet lending pool. Blend gives back **b-tokens** (for example bXLM), which stay in your margin account and count as collateral.

## Supply

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pool">
    On **Farm → Lending/Single Assets**, click **XLM** or **BLUSDC**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    On the **Supply** tab, type an amount or use the % chips. **Margin** shows what your margin account holds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the summary">
    **Source** shows how much comes from borrowed funds and how much from your own collateral (borrowed funds are used first). You also see your deposit before → after, the APY and projected earnings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign">
    Click **Supply XLM to Blend** (or BLUSDC) and approve.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Withdraw

On the **Withdraw** tab, enter the amount in **b-tokens** (what Blend burns). The app converts it to the underlying at the live b-rate and shows what you'll get. A 100% withdrawal re-reads your balance right before sending so nothing is left behind. Click **Withdraw ... to Margin Account**.

Withdrawn tokens go back to your **margin account**, not your wallet. Repaying a loan or transferring to your wallet is a separate step.

## Good to know

- **b-tokens grow in value.** 1 bXLM is worth more than 1 XLM and keeps rising as Blend borrowers pay interest, so your receipt count is smaller than the amount supplied.
- **Blend's rates are Blend's.** On testnet they can be extreme. A high supply APY doesn't guarantee a positive spread after your Vanna borrow rate.
- **Exits can be limited** by Blend's own liquidity.

See [Repay](/stellar/guides/margin/repay) and the [Blend Controller](/stellar/developers/contracts/blend-controller).
