Adding liquidity puts two tokens from your margin account into a Soroswap or Aquarius pool. You get **LP shares** back in the margin account; they earn the pool's trading fees and count as collateral.

| Pool | Tokens |
|---|---|
| Soroswap XLM / SoUSDC | XLM + **SoUSDC** |
| Aquarius XLM / AqUSDC | XLM + **AqUSDC** |

## Add liquidity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pool">
    On **Farm → LP/Multiple Assets**, click the pair.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter one side">
    On **Add Liquidity**, type an amount for either token. The other side fills in automatically from the pool's live price (shown as "1 XLM ≈ … SoUSDC"). **Margin** under each field is what your account holds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the summary">
    **You will receive** (estimated LP shares), **Backed by** (both amounts) and **Share of pool**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign">
    Approve the transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
You need **both** tokens in the margin account. Get SoUSDC or AqUSDC by [borrowing](/stellar/guides/margin/borrow) it, [swapping](/stellar/guides/trade/spot-swap) for it, or [transferring](/stellar/guides/margin/transfer-collateral) it in.
</Tip>

## Remove liquidity

On **Remove Liquidity**, choose how many LP shares to burn (100% uses your exact balance) and sign. Both tokens return to your **margin account**. Removing liquidity doesn't repay debt or send anything to your wallet.

## Good to know

- **Collateral value is conservative.** The Risk Engine values an LP position at twice the smaller side of its share of the reserves, which can be a little less than a DEX shows.
- **Impermanent loss.** If XLM moves against USDC, your LP holds more of the falling token. With borrowed funds, that loss hits your health factor.
- **Liquidation.** If the account is liquidated, LP shares are transferred to the liquidator as they are.

See [Health Factor](/stellar/guides/margin/health-factor).
