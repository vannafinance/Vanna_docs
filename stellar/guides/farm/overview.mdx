**Farm** deploys tokens held in your **margin account**, including borrowed ones, into Stellar DeFi protocols. The position stays inside the margin account, so it keeps counting as collateral under the same health factor.

## The page

- **Stats strip** (connected): **Net Health Factor**, **Your Deposit TVL**, **Your Earnings**, **Net Farm APY** and **Pending Rewards**. Your Earnings and Pending Rewards read $0.00 for now: farm positions have no on-chain cost basis on Stellar yet, and BLND / AQUA reward emissions aren't read by the app.
- **Vaults | Positions**: all pools, or only the ones you're in.
- **LP/Multiple Assets | Lending/Single Assets**: switch between the LP and the Blend lists. Filter by pool, search, or set a threshold.

Click a row to open its pool page: stats on top, **All Transactions | Analytics** on the left (your position chart, position value and history), and the action form on the right.

## Farm vs Earn

| | Earn | Farm |
|---|---|---|
| Funds come from | Your wallet | Your margin account |
| Goes to | Vanna's lending pools | Blend, Soroswap, Aquarius |
| You receive | vTokens in your wallet | A Blend receipt or LP shares in your margin account |
| Counts as margin collateral | No | Yes |

## Before you start

- Your margin account must hold the right tokens. Blend uses **BLUSDC**, Soroswap **SoUSDC** and Aquarius **AqUSDC**; they are not interchangeable. Borrow or swap for the one you need.
- Farmed tokens count as collateral, but they're not free balance. Withdraw from Blend or remove liquidity before you repay or transfer them.

For the full borrow-then-farm loop, see [Leveraged Yield](/stellar/guides/farm/leveraged-yield).
