## Rules for every strategy

- **Use the exact token.** BLUSDC for Blend, SoUSDC for Soroswap, AqUSDC for Aquarius.
- **Check today's rates** on Earn (Borrow APY) and Farm (supply APY) before you start.
- **One account, one health factor.** Every leg in a margin account shares its debt and liquidation risk.
- **Earn vTokens don't back margin.** vTokens in your wallet are not collateral for your margin account.
- **Spot only.** Stellar has no perps or options, so a hedge has to be built from spot positions.
