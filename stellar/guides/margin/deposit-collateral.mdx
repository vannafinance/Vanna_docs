Depositing moves tokens from your **wallet** into your **margin account**, where they back your loans. This is different from supplying on Earn.

There are two ways to deposit:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Deposit and borrow">
    Use **Leverage your Assets** when you want to borrow in the same flow.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Add collateral">
        Under **Deposit**, pick the token. Keep the source on **WB** (wallet balance) and enter an amount or use the % chips. Click **+ Add Collateral** to deposit more than one token.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set the borrow">
        Choose what to borrow and the leverage. Leave leverage at **1x** to deposit without borrowing.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign">
        With one collateral, deposit and borrow go out as **one** transaction. With several collaterals, the app deposits first and borrows second.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    See [Borrow](/stellar/guides/margin/borrow).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Deposit only">
    Use **Transfer Collateral** (or the header **Deposit → Transfer** tab) to add collateral without creating debt.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Pick the direction">
        Set **From** to your wallet and **To** to the margin account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose token and amount">
        Pick the exact token and enter the amount.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Review and sign">
        Check the health factor before → after and sign. Adding collateral only raises your health factor.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    See [Transfer Collateral](/stellar/guides/margin/transfer-collateral).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Good to know

- **Supported collateral:** XLM, BLUSDC, AqUSDC and SoUSDC. The three USDC tokens are different; deposit the one you hold.
- **XLM reserve:** a 100% XLM deposit keeps back your wallet's minimum reserve plus a fee buffer.
- **Check the margin balance**, not only your wallet balance, after the transaction confirms.
