A margin account with debt can be liquidated when its health factor is **1.10 or below**. There is no grace period, and anyone can liquidate it; your signature isn't needed.

<HealthFactorWarning />

## What happens

<Steps>
  <Step title="A liquidator repays all your debt">
    The liquidator pays every loan in full, from their own tokens, back to the Vanna pools.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your Farm positions go to the liquidator">
    Blend supply is withdrawn straight to the liquidator, and Soroswap / Aquarius LP tokens are transferred to them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your remaining tokens go to the liquidator">
    Every token left in the margin account is swept to the liquidator.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Liquidation takes all remaining collateral**, not just the debt plus a bonus. Nothing above the debt comes back to you. The account itself stays open (empty, with no debt) and can be used again.
</Warning>

## How to avoid it

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Watch the buffer" icon="gauge" href="/stellar/guides/margin/health-factor">
    The Liquidation card shows how many dollars you can lose before 1.10. Keep it large.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Repay or add collateral" icon="hand-holding-dollar" href="/stellar/guides/margin/repay">
    Repaying debt or depositing more both raise the health factor straight away.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

- Remember that **interest** keeps adding to your debt, and a stablecoin loan against XLM loses health when XLM falls.
- **Farm positions can't repay a loan directly.** Exit them first, which takes extra transactions; don't leave it to the last minute.
- **Analytics → Liquidations** shows accounts that can be liquidated now and how close others are.

## Settling an account yourself

Separately from liquidation, the contract offers owner-authorized **settlement**: it unwinds Farm positions into the margin account and repays debt from its balances. What's left stays in the account until you withdraw it.
