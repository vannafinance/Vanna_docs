Borrowing happens in the **Leverage your Assets** tab, together with a deposit or against collateral already in the account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the deposit">
    Under **Deposit**, pick a token and amount. **WB** deposits from your wallet. **MB** sizes the loan against collateral already in the margin account, so nothing new is deposited.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Single or Dual Borrow">
    **Single Borrow** puts the whole loan on one asset. **Dual Borrow** splits it 50/50 across two different assets from two pools (you can edit each amount).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the asset">
    Choose XLM, BLUSDC, AqUSDC or SoUSDC. Borrow the USDC variant your venue uses: BLUSDC for Blend, SoUSDC for Soroswap, AqUSDC for Aquarius.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the size">
    Drag the slider (**1x to 10x**), use **− / +**, type an amount, or click **Max Value**. **Total** and **Max** show the loan in USD. Max = deposit × (leverage − 1).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and sign">
    **Transaction Details** shows account leverage, what you're depositing and borrowing, margin collateral, outstanding debt, health factor and liquidation buffer, before → after. Click **Deposit & Borrow** (or **Borrow** in MB mode) and approve.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How many signatures

| Case | Transactions |
|---|---|
| One collateral, Single Borrow | **One**: deposit and borrow together (`deposit_and_borrow`) |
| One collateral, Dual Borrow | **Two**: deposit + first borrow, then the second borrow |
| Several collaterals | Deposits first, then the borrow(s) |
| MB (borrow only) | One borrow per asset |

If a combined deposit-and-borrow would exceed Soroban's per-transaction budget, the app splits it into a deposit and then a borrow. If the second borrow of a Dual Borrow fails, the first one stays done. Check your positions before retrying.

## Where the loan goes

Borrowed tokens land in your **margin account**, not your wallet, and count as collateral there. From there you can [swap](/stellar/guides/trade/spot-swap) or [farm](/stellar/guides/farm/overview). To take them out, use [Transfer Collateral](/stellar/guides/margin/transfer-collateral); that withdrawal is health-checked.

## Limits

- The Risk Engine rejects a borrow unless the account stays **above 1.10** afterward. The app shows the highest leverage that allows, up to 10x.
- A pool lends only the cash it holds, and no borrow can push it past **95% utilization**.
- The app warns you when the health factor after borrowing would sit between 1.10 and 1.25.

## Interest

Debt grows at the pool's **Borrow APY**, which rises with utilization. **Net Borrow Rate** on the Margin page is your account's blended rate. Accrued interest shows in **Positions** and on **Portfolio**.
