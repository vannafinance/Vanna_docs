Your **margin account** is a Soroban smart contract that only you control. It holds your collateral, everything you borrow, and anything you swap or farm with it. One **health factor** covers the whole account.

## The right column

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Margin Account Info** | **Total Borrowed value** (debt with interest), **Net Available Collateral** (holdings minus debt), **Total Value** (everything the account holds), **Net Health Factor**, and **Net Borrow Rate** (your blended borrow rate) |
| **Liquidation** | The health factor, its zone (Safe, Healthy, At risk, Liquidation), the bar from 1.10 to 2.50+, and the dollar **buffer** you can lose before reaching **1.10** |
| **Oracles and LTs** | The on-chain Oracle and Risk Engine contracts, the liquidation threshold and each asset's oracle price |

**Current Positions** shows the whole account as one map, because every deposit backs every loan:

- **Collateral**: what you deposited (borrowed tokens sitting in the account aren't counted twice).
- **Account value**: total holdings, with **Interest accrued** and the **Borrow rate**.
- **Borrowed**: each loan, its share of total debt and a **Repay** button that opens the Repay tab on that asset.
- **Borrowed money is in**: where the borrowed funds sit now, **Farm** (deployed to Blend or LP) or **Idle** (in the account).

**Positions History** lists your margin transactions, each linked to Stellar Expert.
