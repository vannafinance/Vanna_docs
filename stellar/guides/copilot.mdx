**Copilot** is an AI assistant that knows your Vanna account. Ask a question ("What's my health factor?", "Price of XLM") or describe a change ("Deposit 100 XLM and borrow 3x leverage AqUSDC"), and it answers with live figures or prepares the transactions for your wallet to sign.

It does what the rest of the app does, with the same rules and numbers. You just say what you want instead of filling in each page.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Copilot">
    Click **Copilot** in the header with your wallet connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prove your wallet">
    With Freighter, sign a one-time message when asked. It is not a transaction and costs no fee. It lets Copilot read and act only for your own address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask">
    Type in the box at the bottom and press Enter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The sidebar

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **New chat** | Starts a fresh conversation |
| **Health factor** | Your margin account's current health factor and its zone |
| **Auto-approve** | Off by default. See [below](#auto-approve) |
| **Margin Positions** | Each asset your margin account holds or owes |
| **Earn Positions** | What you supplied to Vanna's lending pools |
| **Recents** | Earlier chats. Rename or delete one from its menu |

## What it can do

| You want to | Try |
|---|---|
| **Read your account** | "What's my health factor?", "Show my positions", "Price of XLM" |
| **Lend** | "Supply 500 XLM to Earn", "Withdraw my SoUSDC from Earn" |
| **Use margin** | "Open a margin account", "Deposit 1,000 XLM into my margin account", "Repay all my XLM debt" |
| **Lever up** | "Deposit 100 XLM and borrow AqUSDC at 3x", "Deposit 3,000 XLM, dual-borrow BLUSDC and AqUSDC" |
| **Swap** | "Swap 100 XLM to SoUSDC" (inside your margin account) |
| **Farm** | "Supply 100 XLM to Blend", "Add XLM / SoUSDC liquidity on Soroswap", "Withdraw from Blend" |
| **Withdraw** | "Move 50 XLM from my margin account to my wallet" |

## Approving a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review">
    Copilot restates your request ("Read as: ...") and lists each step with its exact amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve in time">
    A plan is valid for **5 minutes** (the countdown starts at 4:59). After that it expires and you ask again, so it uses fresh prices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign each step">
    Click **Approve** on a step; your wallet opens for that one transaction. **Cancel remaining steps** stops the plan; steps already confirmed stay done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    A step shows as done only after the chain confirms it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every plan is checked against your health factor. Copilot pauses actions that would push the account toward liquidation.

## Auto-approve

Auto-approve lets small, pre-approved steps go through without a confirmation click, within a **per-transaction limit** and a **daily budget** that you set and apply. It is **off** by default. With Freighter, every transaction still opens your wallet to sign; the plan card then says "Auto-approve could not sign this step, so it needs your own signature".

## Assist on every page

The **Assist** tab on the right edge of every page opens a lighter guide. It explains what you're looking at ("What is a health factor?", "How is Earn different from Farm?") and hands you to Copilot when you want to act.
