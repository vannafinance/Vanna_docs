Vanna is a credit layer on Stellar (Soroban). You can lend into Vanna's pools, or open one **margin account**, borrow up to **10x** against it, and use the loan in Stellar DeFi: swaps on **Soroswap** and **Aquarius**, and supply on **Blend**. One health factor covers the whole account.

**Live app:** [test.vanna.finance](https://test.vanna.finance) (choose **Stellar** in the chain switcher at the top right; the first visit asks for an access code)

<TestnetNotice />

## Three steps to start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install a wallet and switch it to Testnet">
    Install [Freighter](https://www.freighter.app/) (OneKey also works) and set its network to **Testnet**. See [Connect Your Wallet](/stellar/guides/connect-wallet).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Open [test.vanna.finance](https://test.vanna.finance), enter your access code if asked, choose **Stellar** in the chain switcher, then click the connect button at the top right and approve the connection in your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mint test tokens">
    Click **Deposit** in the header. Under **Test funds**, press **Mint all**, or **Mint** one token at a time: XLM (Friendbot), BLUSDC (Blend faucet), AqUSDC (Aquarius faucet) and SoUSDC (Soroswap faucet).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The app at a glance

The header groups the pages the way money flows:

| Group | Page | What you do there |
|---|---|---|
| | [Portfolio](/stellar/guides/portfolio) | Net worth, net earnings and health factor; Lender, Trader and Balances tabs |
| **Lend** | [Earn](/stellar/guides/earn/overview) | Supply XLM, BLUSDC, AqUSDC or SoUSDC to Vanna's pools |
| **Borrow & Trade** | [Margin](/stellar/guides/margin/overview) | Deposit collateral, borrow at 1x to 10x, repay, move collateral |
| **Borrow & Trade** | [Trade → Spot](/stellar/guides/trade/spot-swap) | Swap inside your margin account via Soroswap or Aquarius |
| **Borrow & Trade** | [Farm](/stellar/guides/farm/overview) | Supply margin funds to Blend, or add LP on Soroswap / Aquarius |
| | [Analytics](/stellar/guides/analytics/overview) | Protocol-wide risk, read live from the chain |
| | [Copilot](/stellar/guides/copilot) | Ask about your account or describe a change in plain English |
| | Rewards | Marked **Soon** in the header |

The **Deposit** button and your wallet name sit at the top right. A small **Assist** tab on the right edge of every page opens a guide that explains what you're looking at and can hand you over to Copilot.

## Supported assets

| Asset | What it is | Lend (Earn) | Borrow | Margin collateral |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| XLM | Native Stellar lumens | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| BLUSDC | Blend's testnet USDC | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| AqUSDC | Aquarius's testnet USDC | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| SoUSDC | Soroswap's testnet USDC | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

<Warning>
The three USDC tokens are **different contracts**, each priced at about $1. Blend uses BLUSDC, Aquarius uses AqUSDC and Soroswap uses SoUSDC. Borrowing one does not give you another.
</Warning>

Blend supply receipts and Soroswap / Aquarius LP positions held in your margin account also count as collateral.

<Tip>
A wallet signature is not the final result. Wait for the confirmation and refreshed balances before the next step that depends on it.
</Tip>

Continue with [Connect Your Wallet](/stellar/guides/connect-wallet).
