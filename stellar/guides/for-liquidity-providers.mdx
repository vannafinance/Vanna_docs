Use **Earn** to lend from your wallet into a Vanna lending pool. You receive vTokens (vXLM, vBLUSDC, vAqUSDC, vSoUSDC) whose value grows as borrowers pay interest. You don't need a margin account.

## Your path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the token">
    Connect your wallet and mint test tokens from **Deposit → Test funds**. See [Connect Your Wallet](/stellar/guides/connect-wallet).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a pool">
    Open **Earn** and compare Supply APY, utilization and your supply across the four pools.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supply">
    Open the pool, enter an amount under **Supply Liquidity**, check what you get in vTokens and sign. See [Supply](/stellar/guides/earn/supply).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track it">
    Watch your position and earnings on the pool's **Your Positions** tab and on **Portfolio → Lender**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw">
    Redeem vTokens under **Withdraw Liquidity** whenever the pool has cash. See [Withdraw](/stellar/guides/earn/withdraw).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- **Yield is variable.** Supply APY rises and falls with how much of the pool is borrowed.
- **Liquidity can be busy.** You can withdraw only what isn't currently lent out, and one redemption pays at most 50% of the pool's total assets.
- **Your vTokens stay in your wallet.** They are not margin collateral.

See [Earn Overview](/stellar/guides/earn/overview) and [Pool Mechanics](/stellar/learn/lending-pool-mechanics).
