Vanna has two sides that meet in its lending pools:

- **Lenders** supply assets to a Vanna lending pool and receive **vTokens** (vXLM, vBLUSDC, vAqUSDC, vSoUSDC) that grow in value as borrowers pay interest.
- **Traders** open a **margin account**, a personal Soroban smart contract. They deposit collateral, borrow from the pools and use the borrowed funds in Stellar DeFi.

## Follow the tokens

| You do | Tokens move | Where they count |
|---|---|---|
| Supply on **Earn** | Wallet → Vanna lending pool. You get vTokens in your wallet. | Your lending position, not margin collateral |
| Deposit on **Margin** | Wallet → your margin account | Collateral |
| Borrow on **Margin** | Lending pool → your margin account, plus debt | Collateral **and** debt |
| Swap on **Spot** | One token → another, inside the margin account | Still collateral |
| Supply / add LP on **Farm** | Margin account → Blend, Soroswap or Aquarius | Still collateral (as a Blend receipt or LP position) |
| Repay | Margin account → lending pool | Lowers debt |
| Transfer to wallet | Margin account → wallet (health-checked) | Leaves the account |

Borrowed funds never go straight to your wallet. They stay in the margin account, where the protocol can see and value them.

## One account, one health factor

Every position in a margin account (tokens, Blend supply and LP shares) backs every loan in it. The **health factor** compares the account's total collateral value with its total debt:

```text
Health factor = total collateral (USD) / total debt (USD)
```

Above **1.10** the account is healthy. At **1.10 or below** anyone can liquidate it. See [Health Factor](/stellar/guides/margin/health-factor).
