Supplying moves tokens from your **wallet** into a Vanna lending pool. You get vTokens back in your wallet. No margin account is needed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pool">
    Go to **Earn** and click XLM, BLUSDC, AqUSDC or SoUSDC. Make sure your wallet holds that exact token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    Under **Supply Liquidity**, pick the asset, type an amount or use **10% / 25% / 50% / 100%**. Your wallet balance is shown under the field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check More Details">
    Expand **More Details** to see **You Get** (vTokens), the exchange rate (for example XLM per vXLM), the current Supply APY and estimated monthly and yearly earnings at that APY.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign">
    Click **Supply Liquidity** and approve in your wallet. Wait for the confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your position">
    Your vTokens appear on the **Your Positions** tab and in **Portfolio → Lender**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- **You get fewer vTokens than tokens supplied.** Each vToken is already worth more than one unit of the underlying (here 1.0185 XLM per vXLM), so 10 XLM buys about 9.82 vXLM. Their value keeps growing.
- **100% of XLM is safe.** The form keeps back your wallet's minimum XLM reserve plus a fee buffer, so your account can't get stuck.
- **APY isn't fixed.** The estimate uses today's Supply APY, which moves with utilization.
- **vTokens aren't collateral.** They stay in your wallet and don't back a margin account.

History can lag a confirmed transaction by a few seconds. Next: [Withdraw](/stellar/guides/earn/withdraw).
