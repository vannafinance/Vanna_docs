**Earn** (under **Lend** in the header) is where you supply wallet assets to Vanna's lending pools. Margin accounts borrow from these pools and pay interest; that interest flows to lenders through **vTokens**.

## The pools

| Pool | Underlying | Receipt |
|---|---|---|
| XLM | Native XLM | vXLM |
| BLUSDC | Blend's testnet USDC | vBLUSDC |
| AqUSDC | Aquarius's testnet USDC | vAqUSDC |
| SoUSDC | Soroswap's testnet USDC | vSoUSDC |

Each pool has its own liquidity, rates and receipt. The three USDC pools are separate markets for separate tokens.

## The page

- **Dashboard** (when connected): your Overall Deposit and Net Earnings charts.
- **Vaults**: every pool with Assets Supplied, **Supply APY**, Assets Borrowed, **Borrow APY**, **Utilization Rate** and **Your Supply**. Filter by pool, search, or filter by Supply APY.
- **Positions**: only the pools you've supplied to.

Click a pool to open it. A pool page has the stats strip, the **Supply Liquidity / Withdraw Liquidity** form, and five tabs:

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Your Positions** | Your vTokens and what they're worth now |
| **Details** | Total supplied and borrowed, available liquidity, Supply and Borrow APY, utilization, oracle price, vToken exchange rate and supply, contract addresses |
| **Activity** | Your supply and withdraw history |
| **Collateral and Limits** | The account-wide liquidation rule (HF 1.10) and each pool's borrow usage |
| **Analytics** | Pool charts, including the borrow-rate curve read from the on-chain rate model |

## How you earn

Your vToken balance doesn't change, but each vToken is worth more underlying over time as interest accrues. There's nothing to claim. See [vTokens](/stellar/learn/vtokens).
