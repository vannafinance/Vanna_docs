---
title: "Withdraw from Earn"
description: "Redeem vTokens for the underlying asset plus the interest it earned."
mode: "wide"
---

Withdrawing burns your vTokens and sends the underlying, interest included, back to your **wallet**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pool">
    Go to **Earn → Positions** (or the pool) where you hold vTokens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Withdraw Liquidity">
    In the form on the right, choose **Withdraw Liquidity**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter vTokens to redeem">
    The amount is in **vTokens** (for example vXLM), not the underlying. Use **10% / 25% / 50% / 100%**; 100% uses your exact vToken balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign and check">
    Approve in your wallet, then check the underlying received and any vTokens left.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When a withdrawal is limited

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The pool doesn't have enough cash">
    Part of every pool is lent to margin accounts. You can only redeem against **Available Liquidity** (shown on **Details**). If none is available, the transaction fails; try again after borrowers repay or utilization falls.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A large withdrawal fills only partly">
    One redemption pays at most the smallest of: what you asked for, **50% of the pool's total assets**, and the cash available. Only the vTokens for the paid part are burned. The rest stay in your wallet and keep earning; withdraw them in another transaction.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The pool is paused">
    A paused pool stops deposits, withdrawals and new borrows until it is unpaused.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
**Total Supplied** includes money currently lent out. It is not the cash you can withdraw right now; **Available Liquidity** is.
</Note>
