title: "Earn, Borrow, and Hedge"
description: "Keep an Earn position in your wallet while running a separate margin position. An illustrative testnet workflow."
mode: "wide"
---

Supply an asset on **Earn** from your wallet, and separately run a margin position. The two are held and accounted for separately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Supply on Earn">
    Lend XLM or a USDC token from your wallet. See [Supply](/stellar/guides/earn/supply).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the margin leg">
    Deposit collateral and borrow on **Margin**. See [Borrow](/stellar/guides/margin/borrow).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide what you're hedging">
    Write down the exposure you want to offset and which spot tokens offset it. Holding vTokens while borrowing another asset is not a hedge by itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exit each side">
    Redeem vTokens on Earn (a large redemption can fill partly) and unwind / repay the margin leg on its own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Stellar has no perps or options. Any hedge must be built from spot tokens, for example by swapping inside the margin account on Spot.
</Note>

See [Sample Strategies](/stellar/guides/sample-strategies) and [Withdraw](/stellar/guides/earn/withdraw).
