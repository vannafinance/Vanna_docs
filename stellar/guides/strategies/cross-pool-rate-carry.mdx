Borrow a token from Vanna and supply **the same token** to Blend from your margin account. You earn Blend's supply APY on the borrowed amount and pay Vanna's borrow APY on it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deposit and borrow">
    On **Margin → Leverage your Assets**, deposit collateral and borrow **XLM** or **BLUSDC**. See [Borrow](/stellar/guides/margin/borrow).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supply to Blend">
    On **Farm → Lending/Single Assets**, open the same asset and supply it. See [Blend](/stellar/guides/farm/blend-pools).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor the spread">
    Compare Blend's supply APY with the asset's Borrow APY on Earn, and keep an eye on your health factor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exit">
    Withdraw from Blend, [repay](/stellar/guides/margin/repay) the loan in the same asset, then check what's left.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What it does and doesn't hedge

The borrowed and supplied amounts are the same token, so their price moves offset each other. Your own **collateral** is still exposed, both amounts change as interest accrues, and Blend's liquidity and protocol risk remain. The account as a whole is not automatically delta-neutral.
