---
title: "Parallel Rate Carry"
description: "Run XLM and BLUSDC carry legs side by side in one margin account using Dual Borrow."
mode: "wide"
---

Borrow **XLM** and **BLUSDC** together with **Dual Borrow**, then supply each to its matching Blend pool. Both legs live in one margin account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dual Borrow">
    On **Margin → Leverage your Assets**, deposit collateral, switch to **Dual Borrow**, and pick XLM and BLUSDC. See [Borrow](/stellar/guides/margin/borrow).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supply each leg">
    On **Farm**, supply XLM to the Blend XLM pool and BLUSDC to the Blend BLUSDC pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor both spreads">
    Compare each Blend supply APY with its Vanna Borrow APY. Rates, amounts and debt are tracked per asset.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exit both legs">
    Withdraw each asset from Blend and repay its own loan. Then check for leftovers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- **Shared risk.** The two legs share one health factor. They are not two isolated loans.
- **Several transactions.** Opening and closing take several transactions. If one fails after another confirms, check the account before retrying.

See [Sample Strategies](/stellar/guides/sample-strategies) and [Repay](/stellar/guides/margin/repay).
