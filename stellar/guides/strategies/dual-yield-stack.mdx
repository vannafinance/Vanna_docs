Hold an **Earn** position in your wallet while a separate, margin-funded **Blend** supply position runs in your margin account. The two yields come from different places, with different custody, rates and withdrawal limits.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Earn leg">
    Supply on **Earn** from your wallet. See [Supply](/stellar/guides/earn/supply).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Farm leg">
    Deposit and borrow on **Margin**, then supply the borrowed token to Blend on **Farm**. See [Leveraged Yield](/stellar/guides/farm/leveraged-yield).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage each leg">
    The Earn leg is managed on its pool; the Farm leg through the margin account and its health factor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exit">
    Redeem on Earn (it may fill partly). Withdraw from Blend, then repay the loan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- **vTokens don't back the margin leg.** Your Earn position doesn't protect the margin account from liquidation.
- **No guaranteed sum.** The combined yield isn't guaranteed to be positive or price-neutral.

See [Sample Strategies](/stellar/guides/sample-strategies) and [Repay](/stellar/guides/margin/repay).
