**Trade → Spot** swaps tokens that your **margin account** holds, through **Soroswap** or **Aquarius**. The tokens never leave the account, so whatever you receive keeps counting as collateral. Spot is the only trading surface on Stellar; there are no perps or options.

## Venues

| Venue | Pool | USDC token |
|---|---|---|
| **Soroswap** | XLM / SoUSDC | SoUSDC |
| **Aquarius** | XLM / AqUSDC | AqUSDC |

Pick the venue from the **via ▾** menu next to **Swap**. Each venue only lists its own pair, and the token list resets when you switch.

## Swap

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the venue">
    Open **Trade → Spot** and choose **Soroswap** or **Aquarius**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter what you pay">
    Choose the token and amount under **You Pay**, or use the % chips and **Max**. **Swappable** is what the margin account holds of that token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the quote">
    The quote line shows the rate and **HF before → after**. Expand it for the minimum received and slippage. The **Route** card shows the hops and venue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Margin account card">
    It shows health factor, account leverage, borrowed, and swappable balance, each before → after this swap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap">
    Click **Swap** and approve in your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Safety checks

- **Health-factor floor:** if a swap would lower your health factor below **1.15**, the button turns into "Health factor would fall below 1.15" and the swap is blocked. Between 1.15 and 1.25 it shows an amber warning.
- **Slippage:** **Auto** is 0.5%. The settings gear lets you pick 0.1%, 0.5% or 1.0%, or a custom value; above 3% the app warns you.
- **Quotes move.** Prices can change between quote and execution; the minimum received protects you.

<Note>
You need a margin account with tokens in it to swap. Fund it with [Deposit Collateral](/stellar/guides/margin/deposit-collateral) or [Borrow](/stellar/guides/margin/borrow).
</Note>

## History

**Position History** under the swap card lists your margin account's swaps on both venues, newest first. Portfolio → Trader → Spot shows the same list.
